# Tickr Digest — Detailed 15-Second Vertical Video Plan

## Creative direction

- Format: 9:16 vertical
- Audience: busy retail investors
- Tone: editorial premium
- Narrator: warm neutral English
- CTA: “Follow what matters.”
- Use authentic website recordings for all UI.
- Use AI models only for transitions and atmosphere; build the main product animations as editor/keyframed motion.

## Animation language

- Use smooth vertical parallax as the landing page enters frame.
- Animate mint outline boxes around AI briefing, price movement, earnings, and company-news elements.
- Use floating popover labels that slide in, hold briefly, and soften out.
- Add restrained number-count or value-rise motion to price and earnings highlights without changing the recorded values.
- Use a mint data-line sweep to connect the inbox preview to the web digest.
- Add a subtle depth effect by separating browser chrome, digest content, and highlight overlays into layered planes.
- Use one AI-generated transition between the inbox and web digest; all other motion should be deterministic so the interface remains accurate.
- Animate the final logo with a soft mint glow pulse and a short underline draw-on.

## Voiceover script

> “Your watchlist is busy. Your briefing shouldn’t be. Tickr Digest brings AI context, price moves, earnings, and company news into one clear daily view. Follow what matters.”

## Shot script

| Time | Visual | Voiceover / text |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:02 | Tickr Digest logo appears on a dark navy background with a mint line animation. | “Your watchlist is busy.” |

| 0:07–0:09 | Mint data-line sweep leads into one Runway-generated abstract transition, then resolves into the real web digest. | “…price moves…” |
| 0:09–0:11 | Earnings section opens with a controlled card-lift animation; US and India market labels slide into alignment. | “…earnings…” |
| 0:11–0:13 | Real company-news section scrolls into view; source and timestamp receive a brief focus-ring animation. | “…and company news into one clear daily view.” |
| 0:13–0:15 | Logo and CTA end card; underline draws on, mint glow pulses once, and CTA fades up. | “Follow what matters.” |

End card:

- Follow what matters.
- Small secondary CTA: Build my free briefing ↗
- Small disclaimer: AI-generated context. Not investment advice.

## Model plan

- **Runway:** primary model for two short transition variations.
- **Sora 2:** optional premium end-card animation, only if budget permits.
- **Seedence 1.0 Lite:** fallback for inexpensive transition retries.
- Do not use AI generation for readable website screens, prices, ticker symbols, or headlines.
- Use editor/keyframed animation for all UI highlights, popovers, parallax, card lifts, data-line sweeps, and the final logo treatment.
