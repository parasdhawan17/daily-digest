# Tickr Digest

## Product overview

Tickr Digest is a personalized stock-news briefing for people who want a quick, useful view of the companies and funds they follow. It combines an email briefing with a linked web digest so users can scan the important themes quickly and open more detail when they need it.

The service currently supports:

- US stocks and ETFs listed on NYSE and NASDAQ
- NSE-listed Indian equities
- Watchlists that combine US and Indian securities
- Free email briefings and a personalized web digest

Tickr Digest is an information service, not an investment-advice or trading service.

## What the website provides

### Personalized stock briefings

Users choose the tickers they care about. Each briefing is built around that watchlist, so the email and web digest focus on the user's selected stocks instead of a generic market roundup.

### AI market context

The digest includes an AI-generated overview of the main themes across the user's watchlist, plus a focused AI brief for individual stocks. The summaries are generated from the linked headlines and excerpts and are intended to help users understand the bigger picture quickly.

AI output can be incomplete or inaccurate. Users should follow the original source links for full context.

### Company news

Each stock section includes relevant company headlines, excerpts, publishers, timestamps, and links to the original coverage. The email gives users a concise read; the web digest provides the fuller set of company updates.

### Current prices and daily moves

The web digest shows the latest available quote, currency, and daily price movement for each selected stock. US securities are shown in US dollars and Indian securities in rupees.

### Price landmarks

The web digest places the current price in longer-term context, including:

- Distance from the 1-year (52-week) high
- All-time highs for Indian stocks where data is available
- Visual price-range indicators showing how close the current price is to each landmark

### Earnings information

For supported securities, users can view upcoming earnings information and explore recent reported quarters. The earnings view can include EPS, sales, net profit, surprise/beat-or-miss context, and other available results. Data availability varies by company and market.

### Scheduled coverage for two markets

Briefings are scheduled around the trading sessions of the markets a user follows:

| Market | Coverage | Briefing windows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| United States | NYSE and NASDAQ stocks and ETFs | Before the open: 9:15 AM ET; after the close: 4:15 PM ET |
| India | NSE-listed equities | Opening session: 9:15 AM IST; after the close: 3:45 PM IST |

Briefings run on trading days and exclude market holidays. Users receive only the market sessions relevant to their watchlist, with up to two briefings per market they follow.

### Full web digest

Every email includes a personal link to the user's full web digest. The web view brings together the AI overview, stock-level AI briefs, quotes, daily moves, price landmarks, earnings, and company coverage in one place.

The web digest also provides an Edit option so users can update their watchlist.

### Light and dark themes

The website supports light and dark display themes and remembers the user's theme preference in the browser.

## How to sign up

1. Select **Get started free** or **Build my free briefing** on the landing page.
2. Enter an email address.
3. Search for a ticker or company name in the ticker field.
4. Select a validated result, or enter a symbol and press Enter to validate it.
5. Add at least one ticker to the watchlist. US and Indian tickers can be mixed in the same list.
6. Submit the form with **Get your digest**.
7. Check the inbox for the Tickr Digest confirmation email.
8. Click **Confirm subscription** in that email to finish signing up.

No password or separate account setup is required. Email confirmation is used to verify the subscription.

## Updating a watchlist

Existing subscribers can update their tickers without creating a new account:

1. Open **Update tickers** in the website footer, or use **Edit** from the web digest.
2. Enter the same email address used for the subscription.
3. Add or remove validated tickers.
4. Submit the updated watchlist.

The site confirms that the tickers were updated. The new watchlist is used for subsequent briefings.

## Unsubscribing

Users can unsubscribe from the link included in their email briefings. Unsubscribing stops future email delivery.

## What users should know

- The service is currently free to subscribe to.
- At least one valid ticker is required.
- Quotes, earnings, and news depend on source availability and may be delayed or unavailable for some securities.
- AI summaries are a convenience layer and should not be treated as guaranteed fact or investment advice.
- Users should read the linked original reporting and do their own research before making financial decisions.

## Product promise

Tickr Digest helps users spend less time searching across financial news pages and more time understanding the stocks they already follow: a concise briefing in the inbox, with the numbers and context available in the full web digest.
