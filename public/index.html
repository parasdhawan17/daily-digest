Good to know

A few details,
before your first digest.

Your stock briefing starts with an email address and the tickers you care about.

Is Tickr Digest free?

Yes. Your personalized email briefings and web digest are free. Sign up with your email, select your tickers, and confirm your subscription.

What’s in the email versus the web digest?

Your email brings together an AI briefing and company news for your selected stocks. Follow your personal link to the full web digest for quotes and daily moves, 1-year highs, Indian all-time highs, earnings dates and results, and more company coverage. Data availability varies by stock and market.

Can I follow both US and Indian stocks?

Yes. Keep both in one watchlist. US session emails cover your US tickers; India session emails cover your NSE tickers. You receive only the markets you follow.

Can I change my watchlist or unsubscribe?

Use Edit in your web digest, or submit the signup form with the same email to update your tickers. You can unsubscribe using the link in your email.

How should I use the AI summaries and market data?

AI summaries are generated from linked headlines and excerpts and may be incomplete or inaccurate. Quotes and earnings depend on source availability and may be delayed. Read the original sources for context. Tickr Digest provides information, not investment advice.
