Good to know

A few details,
before you sign in.

Your dashboard starts with a Google account and the tickers you care about.

Is Tickr Digest free?

Yes. Your personal stock dashboard is free. Sign in with Google, choose your tickers, and start exploring immediately.

What’s included in my dashboard?

Your dashboard includes watchlist moves, AI context, quotes, 52-week highs, available all-time highs, earnings dates and history, financial health metrics, and sourced company coverage. Data availability varies by stock and market.

Can I follow both US and Indian stocks?

Yes. Keep US stocks, ETFs, and NSE equities together in one watchlist and view them from the same dashboard.

Are email briefings required?

No. Email briefings are optional. If you turn them on, each market’s email covers the stocks you follow around its opening and closing sessions. You can unsubscribe from email without losing your dashboard.

Can I change my watchlist?

Yes. Use Edit in your dashboard to add or remove tickers whenever your interests change.

How should I use the AI summaries and market data?

AI summaries are generated from linked headlines and excerpts and may be incomplete or inaccurate. Quotes and earnings depend on source availability and may be delayed. Read the original sources for context. Tickr Digest provides information, not investment advice.
