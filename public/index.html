    A few things to know

    Good questions.
    Clear answers.

    Everything you need to get started.

    What can I do with Tickr Digest?

    Search Indian companies, read AI summaries, and explore their financial results and recent news. Sign in to save the stocks you follow to a personal watchlist.

    Do I need an account to explore stocks?

    No. Search for a company and explore its research without signing in. A Google sign-in lets you save your watchlist and personalize your dashboard.

    Where does the information come from?

    Company data is provided by IndianAPI. AI summaries bring that data and company news together, with sources available so you can explore the details.

    Are the AI summaries investment advice?

    No. They are a starting point for research. AI summaries may contain mistakes and market data may be delayed. Check the underlying sources before making decisions.
