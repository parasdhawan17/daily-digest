FAQ

Common questions

Is Daily Digest free?

Yes — no subscription fee. Just your email and the US tickers you want news on.

How often will I receive emails?

Twice each US trading day — pre-open at 9:15 AM ET and post-close at 4:15 PM ET. Each briefing covers only the stocks you subscribed to.

How many tickers can I follow?

Up to 10 US-listed stocks or ETFs per subscriber. Pick the tickers you actually watch or own.

Can I change my tickers later?

Yes. Resubmit the form with the same email address to update your tickers. Changes apply on the next session.

What is the live web digest?

A personalized news page with live quotes, session movers, and full headlines for your tickers — refreshed when you open it from email.

Is this a brokerage or portfolio tracker?

No. Daily Digest is a free news service. We don't hold your stocks, track your portfolio, or give investment advice — we email and publish news on the US tickers you choose.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Your email links to your tickers. Subscribe, confirm if prompted, and you're set.
